Reputation & sentiment classification on news

Benchmarked 8 transformer models (LLaMA-7B, Flan-T5, BERT variants, XLM-RoBERTa) on German news for multilabel reputation and sentiment. Applied 4-bit quantization and L1 pruning to LLaMA-7B, and used DSPy for prompt optimisation. Fine-tuned smaller models beat the larger LLMs.
